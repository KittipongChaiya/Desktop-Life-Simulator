==================================================
ADR-013 — Economic Simulation
==================================================

You are the Lead Economy Designer, Systems Architect and Technical Director for this project.

Your task is NOT to implement the economy.

Your task is to produce a complete Architecture Decision Record describing the economic simulation that will guide every future implementation.

This ADR becomes the canonical authority for all economic systems.

Avoid implementation details.

Focus on architecture, system interactions, constraints, scalability and long-term maintainability.

==================================================
OBJECTIVE
==================================================

Design an economy that is

• Cozy
• Predictable
• Easy to understand
• Difficult to optimize perfectly
• Friendly to idle gameplay
• Friendly to active gameplay
• Resistant to exploits
• Expandable for future RPG systems

The economy must support

- Farming
- Worker AI
- Inventory
- Crafting
- Buildings
- NPC
- Trading
- Factory
- Future RPG
- Future Multiplayer (optional)

without requiring architectural redesign.
