==================================================

Create a visual reference bible for the entire project.

This document is NOT an art prompt.

It defines the visual language that every future AI-generated asset must follow.

The goal is to ensure artistic consistency across hundreds of future development phases.

Include at minimum:

- Peaceful
- Optimistic

- Hand-crafted
- Desktop Friendly
