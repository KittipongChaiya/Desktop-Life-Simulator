# STYLE_LOCK

> **Status:** Immutable. The highest creative authority for asset generation. Changing a rule here is a project-level decision, not an art choice.
> **Owns:** The binding "never do this" rules that prevent visual drift across phases and sessions.
> **Does not own:** The values the rules reference — sizes (`PIXEL_GUIDE.md`), colours (`COLOR_PALETTE.md`), the build pipeline (`ASSETS.md`). This file forbids; those files specify.

**This is the most important document in `docs/assets/`.** Every future AI session that generates any visual asset must read it and obey it. When a generation request conflicts with a rule here, the rule wins — no exceptions without an explicit, recorded change to this file. The rules are numbered `R-xx` so other documents and reviews can cite them precisely.

The reason a style lock exists: art in this project is generated by many independent sessions over a long time. Consistency cannot come from a shared memory that does not exist (`VISION.md §2.5`) — it can only come from a written, enforced contract. This is that contract.

---

## How to read a rule

Each rule is a **prohibition** with a **because**. The prohibition is binding; the because is why, so a future session understands the cost of breaking it and does not "improve" the style into incoherence.

---

## A. Rendering & fidelity

**R-01 — Do NOT use realistic or semi-realistic rendering.** The style is flat, hand-crafted pixel art. _Because_ the identity is cozy and charming (`VISION.md §2`), and realism reads as cold and raises the art bar beyond what a content library can sustain.

**R-02 — Do NOT use anti-aliasing, soft alpha, gradients, or blur.** Silhouette alpha is hard-edged (fully opaque or fully transparent); shading is stepped through named ramp colours, never a smooth gradient. _Because_ nearest-neighbour scaling and `antialias: false` are set globally (`ASSETS.md §8`); soft edges shimmer and blur under integer zoom and defeat the outline.

**R-03 — Do NOT outline in pure black (`#000000`).** The one outline colour is `#3A3640` (`COLOR_PALETTE.md §2`). _Because_ black is harsh and cold and breaks the warm palette; the chosen outline is dark enough to read, warm enough to belong.

---

## B. Perspective & form

**R-04 — Do NOT vary outline thickness.** Exactly **1 px** at 1×, on every entity and object, everywhere (`PIXEL_GUIDE.md §5`). _Because_ variable line weight is the single most obvious tell of mixed-source art; uniform 1 px is what makes independently-authored sprites look like one hand made them.

**R-05 — Do NOT mix perspectives.** Ground is flat top-down; standing objects use the one shallow 3/4 tilt (`PIXEL_GUIDE.md §6`, `ASSETS.md §2`). No true isometric, no vanishing-point perspective, no front-elevation buildings among top-down ones. _Because_ a scene that mixes projections cannot be made to read as one coherent space at a glance.

**R-06 — Do NOT change the light direction.** One global soft light from the **upper-left** (`PIXEL_GUIDE.md §7`). _Because_ inconsistent lighting makes assets look pasted together; a single light is the cheapest way to unify a whole library.

**R-07 — Do NOT change established proportions.** Character head-to-body ratio, height classes, and silhouette rules are fixed in `CHARACTER_BIBLE.md` (phase-05.5b). _Because_ proportion is identity — a worker the player recognises in one frame must be the same shape in every frame and every phase.

---

## C. Colour

**R-08 — Do NOT introduce a colour outside `COLOR_PALETTE.md`.** A new colour is a reviewed change to that file, never an in-sprite choice (`COLOR_PALETTE.md §10`). _Because_ a shared limited palette is the guarantee that core and plugin art harmonise (`ASSETS.md §2`); one rogue hue starts the drift.

**R-09 — Do NOT spend reserved accents on decoration.** Reward Gold, Rare Violet, and Danger Red carry meaning (`COLOR_PALETTE.md §4`). _Because_ an accent used everywhere signals nothing; the instant read of "reward" or "rare" depends on the colour being rare.

**R-10 — Do NOT use Danger Red for routine or negative-but-normal feedback in v0.1.** Legality and state are shown green/amber (`GAME_DESIGN.md §10.1`, `COLOR_PALETTE.md §5`). _Because_ the game rewards absence and never punishes inattention (`VISION.md §2.2`); training the player to feel alarm is the opposite of the product.

---

## D. Animation

**R-11 — Do NOT use inconsistent animation timing.** Timing is in simulation ticks, not milliseconds, tied to game time (`ASSETS.md §7`); frame counts follow `PIXEL_GUIDE.md §8` / `ANIMATION_GUIDE.md`. _Because_ display-refresh-based timing desyncs from the deterministic simulation (`ADR-007`) and makes motion feel different on different machines.

**R-12 — Do NOT leave an animation playing off-screen.** Every playing animation registers and releases `animatingEntityCount` and is paused when culled (`ASSETS.md §7.1`). _Because_ a looping off-screen animation is a permanent, invisible CPU cost — the highest-risk way to break the idle budget (`PERFORMANCE.md`), and the idle budget is a product feature (`VISION.md §2.1`).

---

## E. UI

**R-13 — Do NOT change the UI style per panel.** All UI follows `UI_STYLE_GUIDE.md` (phase-05.5c) and draws from the UI palette (`COLOR_PALETTE.md §6`). _Because_ the overlay is one small surface (`VISION.md §2.3`); inconsistent panels make a tiny UI feel chaotic.

**R-14 — Do NOT convey state by hue alone.** Pair colour with shape/icon/value (`COLOR_PALETTE.md §9`). _Because_ ~8% of players cannot rely on hue; readability is accessibility.

---

## F. Density & scale

**R-15 — Do NOT mix pixel densities.** All art is authored at the 32 px grid at 1× (with a 2× variant), never up- or down-scaled by non-integer factors, never blending 16-grid and 32-grid art in one scene (`PIXEL_GUIDE.md §1`, `ASSETS.md §8`). _Because_ mixed densities make some sprites look blurry or chunky beside others and shatter the illusion of one world.

**R-16 — Do NOT add detail that dies at gameplay zoom.** If a detail is illegible at 1× on the overlay, it is noise (`QUALITY_GUIDELINES.md`). _Because_ the game is glanced at (`VISION.md §1`); readability beats richness every time.

---

## G. Process

**R-17 — Do NOT generate assets outside the established art direction.** Every asset conforms to `ART_DIRECTION.md`, this file, and the guides it references. _Because_ the whole point of this documentation set is that generation is _constrained_, not invented per session.

**R-18 — Do NOT add an asset without attribution.** Every asset carries provenance (`ASSETS.md §11`). _Because_ unclear provenance is a release-blocking liability that is impossible to reconstruct later.

---

## The one-line test

Before shipping any asset, a session asks: _"Would a player believe the same hand made this and the storage shed?"_ If not, a rule above is being broken — find which one. This test is expanded into a checklist in `QUALITY_GUIDELINES.md`.
